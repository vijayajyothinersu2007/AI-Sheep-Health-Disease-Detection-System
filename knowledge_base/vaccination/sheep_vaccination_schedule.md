## Recommended Annual Flock Vaccination Calendar

| Month / Timing | Disease Target | Vaccine Type | Dose & Route | Target Age / Stock | Notes & Precaution |

| **May – June (Pre-Monsoon)** | **Enterotoxemia (ET) / Pulpy Kidney** | Inactivated Alum Precipitated Vaccine | 2.5 ml SC | All sheep > 2 months; annual booster | Essential before monsoon green flush and sudden feed shifts. |
| **June – July (Pre-Monsoon)** | **Haemorrhagic Septicaemia (HS) / Domma** | Alum Precipitated / Oil Adjuvant Vaccine | 2.0 ml SC | All sheep > 3 months; annual | Protects against lethal monsoon Pasteurella outbreaks. |

## Strategic Deworming (Anthelmintic) Calendar
- **Pre-Monsoon (May):** Broad-spectrum deworming (Albendazole or Fenbendazole) to clear adult tapeworms and roundworms before moisture accelerates larvae on pastures.
- **Mid-Monsoon (August):** Target blood-sucking barber's pole worm (*Haemonchus contortus*) using Closantel or Levamisole based on pale eye conjunctiva (FAMACHA scoring).
- **Post-Monsoon (November):** Treat against liver flukes (*Fasciola*) using Triclabendazole if sheep grazed near irrigation tanks or damp lowlands.
- **Rule of Anthelmintic Rotation:** Rotate the class of dewormer annually to delay the emergence of resistant internal parasites.

## Flock Biosecurity & Quarantine Protocols
1. **The 30-Day Quarantine Rule:** Keep all newly acquired, market-bought, or returned sheep in a separate quarantine pen at least 15 meters away from the main flock for 21 to 30 days. Observe daily for fever, mouth ulcers, coughing, or diarrhoea before merging.
2. **Visitor & Vehicle Disinfection:** Place wheel baths or lime dry powder strips at farm gates. Limit unauthorized visitors into lambing pens.
3. **Flock Isolation During Local Outbreaks:** At the first report of an outbreak in the mandal, immediately cease communal grazing on village common lands and prevent flock mixing at watering points.
