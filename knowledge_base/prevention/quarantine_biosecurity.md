## Sick Animal Isolation Pen Protocols
1. **Physical Location:** The sick quarantine shed must be located downwind, at least 15 to 20 meters away from healthy sheep pens and lambing yards.
2. **Dedicated Equipment:** Dedicate separate feed troughs, water buckets, brushes, and cleaning tools exclusively for the isolation pen. Never use sick pen utensils in healthy sheds.
3. **Attendant Protocol:** Always feed, clean, and tend to healthy flocks first, and tend to sick animals in isolation last. Wear dedicated rubber boots or step in a disinfectant footbath before entering and upon exiting the isolation pen.
4. **Ventilation & Floor Hygiene:** Isolation pens must be dry, well-ventilated, and protected from cold night winds and harsh midday sun. Elevated wooden slatted flooring or dry gravel covered with clean straw is ideal.

## Chemical Disinfection Guidelines
- **Bleaching Powder (Chlorinated Lime):** Sprinkle dry bleaching powder daily around shed perimeters, drain lines, and entryways.
- **Sodium Hydroxide (Caustic Soda 2%):** Highly effective virucidal wash for pens and walls after an outbreak of Sheep Pox, PPR, or FMD.
- **Slaked Lime (Calcium Hydroxide):** Whitewash walls every 3-4 months with slaked lime containing 1% copper sulphate or carbolic acid to repel ticks and mites.
- **Potassium Permanganate (1:1000):** Non-toxic antiseptic wash for feed and water troughs.

## Vector & Fly Strike Suppression
- Keep manure heaps at least 50 meters away from sheep shelters to eliminate fly breeding sites.
- Apply safe pyrethroid or neem-based fly repellent sprays inside shelters to reduce biting flies (*Stomoxys*) that mechanically transmit Sheep Pox and bacteria.
