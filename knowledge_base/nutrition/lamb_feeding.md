## Colostrum: The Golden First Milk
Colostrum is vital for lamb survival as lambs are born without protective maternal antibodies (agammaglobulinemic).
- **First 2 Hours Rule:** Lambs must receive warm colostrum within the first 1 to 2 hours of birth. Gut closure begins rapidly and antibody absorption drops to near zero after 24 hours.
- **Volume Required:** Feed at least 50 ml colostrum per kg body weight in the first 2 hours, and total 200 ml/kg body weight within the first 24 hours.
- **Weak Lambs:** If the lamb is too weak to suckle, warm colostrum must be administered using a sterile stomach tube or feeding syringe.

## Creep Feeding Strategy
Creep feeding provides grain supplements exclusively to suckling lambs while barring adult ewes:
- **Commence at 2 to 3 Weeks:** Introduce high-protein (18-20% crude protein) creep pellets or coarse crumble.
- **Creep Mixture Formulation:**
  - Crushed yellow maize: 50%
  - Soybean meal: 30%
  - Wheat bran: 17%
  - Mineral mixture: 2%
  - Salt: 1%
- **Benefits:** Accelerates early rumen papillae development, speeds up daily weight gain (ADG), and reduces weaning stress.

## Weaning Protocol
- Lambs can be safely weaned at 90 days of age if they are consuming at least 250 grams of dry creep feed daily.
- Provide clean, sweet hay (oat hay or legume hay) alongside concentrate. Ensure unlimited access to fresh, cool drinking water.
