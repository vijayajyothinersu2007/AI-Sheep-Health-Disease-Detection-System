## Nutritional Stages in Pregnant Sheep
Sheep gestation lasts approximately 147–150 days (5 months). Nutritional management is divided into:
1. **Early Gestation (Days 1–90):** Nutrient demands are similar to maintenance. Excessive fattening or severe underfeeding must be avoided.
2. **Late Gestation (Days 90–150):** Critical phase. Over 70% of fetal growth occurs in the last 6 weeks. The growing uterus compresses the rumen, reducing dry matter intake by up to 20-30% just when nutrient demand surges.

## Late Gestation Diet Formulation ('Steaming Up')
To prevent Pregnancy Toxemia (Twin Lamb Disease) and hypocalcemia (milk fever):
- **Concentrate Supplementation:** Feed 250 to 400 grams of energy-dense concentrate mixture per ewe daily, starting 4 to 6 weeks before lambing.
- **Concentrate Mixture Composition:**
  - Maize / Sorghum / Broken grain: 40%
  - Wheat bran / Rice polish: 30%
  - Soybean meal / Groundnut cake: 27%
  - Mineral mixture: 2%
  - Common salt: 1%
- **High-Quality Green Forage:** Provide 3 to 4 kg of leguminous green fodder (Lucerne, Berseem, Cowpea) or sweet sorghum.
- **Energy Drenching for Lethargic Ewes:** If ewes show hesitation or dullness, provide 50 ml of liquid glycerol/propylene glycol daily to supply instant glucose precursors.

## Mineral and Vitamin Support
- **Calcium and Phosphorus:** Ensure calcium-to-phosphorus ratio of 2:1. Deficiencies lead to post-parturient recumbency and uterine inertia.
- **Trace Minerals:** Zinc, Copper, Selenium, and Vitamin E reduce stillbirths and retain lamb vitality (preventing White Muscle Disease).
