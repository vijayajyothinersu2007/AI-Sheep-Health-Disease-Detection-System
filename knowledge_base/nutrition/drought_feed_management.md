## Survival Feeding Principles in Drought
During severe fodder deficits, the objective shifts from production to maintenance and flock preservation.

## Utilization of Fodder Tree Leaves (Top Feeds)
Deep-rooted perennial trees remain green during prolonged drought:
- **Subabul (Leucaena leucocephala):** High protein fodder. Do not exceed 25-30% of total diet due to mimosine toxicity.
- **Neem (Azadirachta indica):** Excellent dry season supplement; possesses mild anti-parasitic properties.
- **Babool / Kikar (Acacia nilotica) pods and leaves:** Rich in energy and crude protein (12-14%).
- **Prosopis cineraria (Khejri):** Premium traditional desert fodder in arid zones.

## Urea Ammoniation of Crop Straws (Paddy/Wheat Straw)
- Dry cereal straw has low crude protein (3-4%) and poor digestibility.
- **4% Urea Treatment Technique:** Dissolve 4 kg fertilizer-grade urea in 40-50 liters of water. Spray uniformly over 100 kg chopped dry straw. Stack and seal airtight under polythene sheet for 21 days (tropical climate).
- **Result:** Crude protein increases to 8-9%, digestibility improves by 10-15%, making it a viable maintenance roughage.

## Mineral Licks and Molasses Blocks
- Provide Urea-Molasses-Mineral Blocks (UMMB) in sheds for controlled licking. Licking stimulates rumen microbes to digest fibrous dry roughage without risk of urea poisoning.
- Water must never be restricted when feeding urea or mineral supplements.
